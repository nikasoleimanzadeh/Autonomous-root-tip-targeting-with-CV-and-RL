# V5.1 development evidence — root-start refinement (Task 8)

## Why I iterated

The Task 5 U-Net predicts root versus background pixels, but its binary mask does not tell me where each individual plant starts. My first root-origin detector estimates likely start coordinates from the predicted root mask. This approach can produce false starts too low in the image and can miss small roots close to the plant row.

## Iteration 1 — Baseline root starts to V5.1

I developed **V5.1 as a traditional computer-vision refinement**, not a separate neural-network model. First, `detect_root_origins(mask)` produces initial start coordinates. Next, `refine_root_starts_v51(mask, preliminary_starts)` estimates the likely plant row, removes starts that are too far from that row, searches a restricted band for small vertical root components, and combines near-duplicate candidates. Original accepted starts take priority when candidates overlap.

The function is implemented directly in [Task 8 / task_8.ipynb](https://github.com/BredaUniversityADSAI/2025-26b-fai2-adsai-nikasoleimanzadeh244258/blob/main/Task%208/task_8.ipynb) under **Root-start localization — V5.1**, in the root-start detection and refinement code cell. The following code cell saves both baseline `detected_root_starts.csv` and refined `v51_root_starts.csv`. The subsequent sanity-check cell visualizes the refined start positions.

## Saved comparison (19-image Task 8 run)

| Measurement | Before | After V5.1 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Total retained root starts | 86 | 87 |

V5.1 identified **8 rescue candidates across 6 images** in the saved notebook output. The final total increased by just one because filtering and candidate merging also removed or consolidated detections. This shows a change in detected roots, **not independently verified detection accuracy**.

These refined coordinates become anchors for the Task 6 root assignment, which feeds the Task 7 primary-root tracing. The end-to-end notebook processed 19/19 images and produced 87 measured root records (76 `OK`, 11 flagged for review). See [Task 8 README](https://github.com/BredaUniversityADSAI/2025-26b-fai2-adsai-nikasoleimanzadeh244258/blob/main/Task%208/README.md) for limitations and [Kaggle evidence](https://github.com/BredaUniversityADSAI/2025-26b-fai2-adsai-nikasoleimanzadeh244258/blob/main/Task%208/evidence/kaggle_private_leaderboard.png) for the 16.159% final private sMAPE and 4th position.

## Limits

V5.1 thresholds were selected during development and may not generalize to a different image layout, resolution, or plant species. The pipeline ran end-to-end in the saved test batch, but this alone does not prove accurate identification of all root starts.
